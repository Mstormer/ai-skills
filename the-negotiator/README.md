# The Negotiator

A Claude skill that coaches you through a negotiation using Chris Voss's Black Swan methodology. Share a situation or an email chain; it analyzes it, proposes a strategy, drafts or improves your message (accusations audit, calibrated questions, "No"-oriented questions, loss aversion, Ackerman bargaining), and explains why each part works. The methodology summary it draws on is in [references/negotiation-details.md](references/negotiation-details.md).

## Install

Copy this folder, including `references/`, into `~/.claude/skills/` (Claude Code) or `~/.agents/skills/` (Codex), or upload it as a skill in Claude. Then say: "Use The Negotiator to improve my reply to this email."

## Privacy

Don't share information you wouldn't want sent to the AI service you use.
