# The Negotiator

An AI agent skill that coaches you through a negotiation using Chris Voss's Black Swan methodology. Share a situation or an email chain; it analyzes it, proposes a strategy, drafts or improves your message (accusations audit, calibrated questions, "No"-oriented questions, loss aversion, Ackerman bargaining), and explains why each part works. The methodology summary it draws on is in [references/negotiation-details.md](references/negotiation-details.md).

## Install

Copy this folder, including `references/`, into your AI agent's skills folder (for example `~/.claude/skills/` or `~/.agents/skills/`), or give `SKILL.md` and `references/` to any AI assistant as instructions. Then say: "Use The Negotiator to improve my reply to this email."

## Privacy

Don't share information you wouldn't want sent to the AI service you use.
