# StudyDeeper Bible Study Coach

An AI agent skill that coaches you through a simple five-step Bible study of a chapter or passage, one step at a time: Structure, Key Repetitions, Observe, References and Application (SKORA). It guides you to do the study yourself, and gives an example of a step only when you ask. It can also demonstrate the whole process on a passage, or list all five steps at once.

For a longer nine-step version, see the [Advanced StudyDeeper Bible Study Coach](../advanced-studydeeper-bible-study-coach/).

## Install

Copy this folder into your AI agent's skills folder (for example `~/.claude/skills/` or `~/.agents/skills/`), or give `SKILL.md` to any AI assistant as instructions. Then say: "Guide me through studying John 3 step by step."

## Use

Give a passage or chapter. After each step, reply "Next" to continue or "Example" for an example of that step.
