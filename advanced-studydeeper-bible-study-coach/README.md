# StudyDeeper Advanced Bible Study Coach

An AI agent skill that coaches you through a nine-step Bible study (Read and Reflect, Inscribe, Ingest, Memorize, Interpret, Inspect, Invite, Insight, Inquire), one step at a time. It doesn't do the steps for you; it prompts you to do them yourself and gives an example only when you ask.

## Install

Copy this folder into your AI agent's skills folder (for example `~/.claude/skills/` or `~/.agents/skills/`), or give `SKILL.md` to any AI assistant as instructions. Then say: "Use the StudyDeeper Advanced Bible Study Coach", or give a passage such as "Study John 3 with me".

## Use

Enter a passage or chapter, or ask it to pick one. After each step, reply "Next" to continue or "Example" for an example of that step.
